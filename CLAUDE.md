@DAPHNE_MEREL_DESIGN_CODEBOOK.md

Before making any visual, layout, typography, spacing, color, navigation, artwork-presentation, or interaction change:

1. Read DAPHNE_MEREL_DESIGN_CODEBOOK.md completely.
2. Treat it as the single source of truth for the visual design of the Daphne Merel artist website.
3. Do not invent new colors, fonts, spacing values, corner radii, shadows, or UI styles unless explicitly requested.
4. Preserve artwork aspect ratios and keep artwork visually dominant.
5. Do not use gradients, generic rounded cards, unnecessary shadows, SaaS-style layouts, or excessive animation.
6. Keep the site editorial, gallery-like, asymmetrical, spacious, and image-led.
7. Do not invent artist biography, artwork metadata, exhibitions, galleries, dates, materials, dimensions, or contact information.
8. Reuse the approved design tokens and existing content structure.
9. For significant visual changes, explain the intended change first and wait for approval before pushing to main.
